# Reviewer Invitation, Application, and Selection Mechanism

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](REVIEWER_RECRUITMENT_AND_SELECTION.md)

> Status: Public discussion draft<br>
> Version: v0.2-draft<br>
> Formal application status: -

This mechanism governs two entry routes: proactive project invitations to experts, and voluntary applications by experts and workers. Invitations and applications follow the same eligibility, verification, conflict-of-interest, privacy, and feedback rules. Being invited does not mean one's opinions are endorsed, and applying voluntarily does not lower one's eligibility.

---

## I. Channel Separation

### 1.1 Public Expressions of Interest

The project may use GitHub Issues to collect public expressions of interest without personal information. GitHub usernames and content remain publicly available over time, so public expressions of interest:

- Are not formal applications;
- Do not complete identity verification;
- Do not require private contact details, actual affiliations, identity documents, or proof of work;
- Allow workers to submit true cases they personally experienced, directly know about, or have reasonable factual grounds for, and choose how to identify themselves publicly; unauthorized affiliations, third-party information, internal materials, or private receipts must not be published;
- Serve only to understand the scope of participation and provide next-step instructions after a nonpublic channel opens.

### 1.2 Formal Nonpublic Applications

Formal applications use [EXPERT_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md](EXPERT_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md) or [WORKER_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md](WORKER_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md), and may be submitted only through the nonpublic channel designated in the round announcement.

Formal applications remain closed until the responsible entity, receiving channel, service providers, storage regions, authorized users, and deletion arrangements in [PRIVACY_NOTICE.en.md](PRIVACY_NOTICE.en.md) are completed. The existing safety-report email does not automatically become an application email.

## II. Proactive Expert Invitations

### 2.1 Candidate Sources

- Proposed by the project lead;
- Recommended by confirmed participants;
- Experts publicly expressing interest;
- Proactive project searches to fill gaps in professional, industry, regional, or methodological coverage.

Do not purchase lists, scrape private contact details, or screen for agreement with the project.

### 2.2 Confirmation Before Invitation

Create a nonpublic candidate check record for each prospective expert:

1. The project lead determines the expertise and seats needed for the round;
2. Check the candidate's publicly available relevant experience, verifiability, public indications of conflicts, value for coverage, and information gaps;
3. The project lead checks sources and decides “approve invitation / request more information / do not invite”;
4. Defer where significant concerns exist; seek review by an unconflicted person where necessary;
5. Before sending, record whether the contact source is lawful, whether contact might constitute harassment, and who will send it;
6. Do not send bulk invitations or make automated contact without individual confirmation by the project lead.

### 2.3 Invitation Content

Use [EXPERT_INVITATION_TEMPLATE.en.md](EXPERT_INVITATION_TEMPLATE.en.md), specifying at least:

- Why the person is invited and what they are asked to review;
- Frozen version, schedule, expected burden, and feedback date;
- Whether compensation is available;
- The right to decline, review only part, or keep their name private;
- Verification, conflict-of-interest, and privacy boundaries;
- That invitation does not endorse their views or require support for the draft.

## III. Expert Application Mechanism

### 3.1 Basic Requirements

- Relevant knowledge or long-term practical experience addressing at least one professional issue in the round;
- Willingness to explain the regions, industries, periods, and boundaries to which judgments apply;
- Ability to provide one low-risk, reasonably verifiable public background source or alternative method;
- Willingness to disclose relevant drafting, employment, management, ownership, funding, contractual, co-authorship, dispute, and direct-benefit relationships;
- Acceptance that comments may not be adopted and identity verification does not increase their weight.

Education, professional title, institutional prestige, political position, invitation status, and agreement with the draft are not prerequisites. Practitioners may apply to the expert track.

### 3.2 Processing Workflow

> Public expression of interest (optional) → Nonpublic application → Initial privacy and completeness screening → Lightweight background verification → Conflict-of-interest classification → Relevance and coverage assessment → Project lead's decision → Notification → Appointment to a specific round

Application outcomes: Awaiting additional information; entered into candidate pool; approved pending appointment; approved for specified topics only; barred from decision-making seats due to major conflicts; not approved; withdrawn. Entry into the candidate pool does not guarantee appointment in every round.

### 3.3 Decision Rules

The project lead bears final responsibility for admission. Decisions must state relevance to the round's tasks, verification status, conflict restrictions, coverage considerations, and term. “We think they are suitable” alone is insufficient.

## IV. Worker Application Mechanism

### 4.1 Basic Requirements

- Current or past direct experience of work processes, including people who are unemployed, retired, away from work due to injury, or in career transition;
- Willingness to describe relevant work experience using broad categories only;
- Ability to choose a low-risk verification method, or acceptance of participation through unverified comments;
- Ability to participate under conditions they consider safe and determine their own review scope.

No education, professional title, GitHub, technical terminology, complete identity documents, employment contracts, payslips, or evidence of rights claims is required. Frontline management, self-employment, or multiple roles do not automatically exclude applicants, but they should explain their primary role for this round and relevant power relationships.

### 4.2 Processing Workflow

> Public expression of interest (optional) → Nonpublic application → Initial safety and contact-preference screening → Minimal work-experience verification → Retaliation-risk check → Coverage and assistance-needs assessment → Project lead's decision → Notification → Appointment to a specific round

People unable or unwilling to undergo verification may still submit ordinary public or nonpublic comments, but are not counted among formally verified worker reviewers. Low scores, strong criticism, refusal of attribution, or needing someone to record their comments are never grounds for rejection.

### 4.3 Selection and Burden

When capacity is limited, select according to the round's industries, employment forms, regions/languages, AI impacts, and existing coverage gaps, rather than first-come-first-served or viewpoint screening. Publish capacity, expected time requirements, assistance methods, and subsidies. Unselected applicants may enter the next round's candidate pool or submit comments only, without implying that their experience lacks value.

---

## V. Shared Scrutiny and Recusal

Inviters, application handlers, and verifiers should disclose management, funding, contractual, co-authorship, close personal relationships, or direct disputes with candidates. Candidates cannot determine their own conflict level alone, and the project lead cannot handle alone an application involving a significant relationship with themselves. Defer if no independent handler is available.

An applicant may have both expert and worker backgrounds, but must choose one primary track for each principle in each round. They cannot hold two adjudication seats or be counted twice.

---

## VI. Service Targets and Feedback

| Stage | Target timeframe |
| --- | --- |
| Receipt acknowledgment | 3 working days |
| Initial privacy and completeness screening | 7 working days |
| Additional-information deadline | 10 calendar days after notice |
| Application outcome | 20 working days after receipt of a complete application |
| Procedural reconsideration request | 10 working days after delivery of the outcome |
| Reconsideration response | 20 working days after receipt of a complete request |

Outcome notices should state status, main reasons, minimum additional information needed, publication/retention arrangements, candidate-pool inclusion, and the reconsideration route. Ordinary public expressions of interest can check status through public links; applicants must not be required to paste private receipts into GitHub.

---

## VII. Records and Publication

Public summaries report only application counts, source types, broad professional/work categories, statuses such as approved/awaiting additional information/withdrawn, and coverage gaps that cannot enable re-identification. Names, contact details, precise affiliations, supporting links, private receipts, internal scores, and rejection details are nonpublic by default.

Publication of a person's “verified expert/worker” status, name, background, affiliation, and specific round requires separate authorization for each item. Application-data retention, deletion, and rights requests follow [PRIVACY_NOTICE.en.md](PRIVACY_NOTICE.en.md).

---

## VIII. Suspension Conditions

Stop accepting formal applications if any of the following occurs:

- The nonpublic channel, responsible entity, access permissions, or deletion mechanism is not configured;
- Processing demand exceeds published capacity;
- The sole decision-maker has an unavoidable major conflict concerning a candidate;
- Application materials are accidentally published or a security incident occurs;
- Promised feedback, assistance, or protection against retaliation cannot be provided;
- Application rules materially change without renewed notification.

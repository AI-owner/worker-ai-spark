This file records only public processing information with item-by-item publication authorization that has passed re-identification checks. Do not enter names, contact details, precise affiliations, positions, private receipt numbers, verification materials, private supporting links, or internal verification notes.

Comments intended for internal processing only, with metadata not authorized for publication, or whose publication might expose identities must not receive individual public records. Reflect them only as aggregate counts and themes in round summaries that cannot enable re-identification.

## II. Field Rules

- **Public record sub-item number**: WAI-PR-round-type-sequence-I-sub-item, with no identity meaning and distinct from the private receipt number;
- **Track authorized for publication**: Enter EX expert or WK worker only with the participant's separate consent and when safe; without authorization, do not create an individual record;
- **Target version / commit**: Must point to frozen text; retain “awaiting additional information” if it cannot be confirmed;
- **Provision**: Principle number, section, or “overall structure”;
- **Process status**: Received, privacy suspension, awaiting additional information, valid formal review, ordinary public comment, under deliberation, deferred, withdrawn, duplicate merged, invalid, closed;
- **Handling outcome**: Adopted, partially adopted, to enter the next version, additional materials needed, targeted research needed, dispute unresolved, handling deferred, not adopted, out of scope, unable to verify, invalid submission;
- **Maturity/evidence status**: Maintainer draft, process pilot review, insufficient evidence, dual-track review completed—dispute unresolved, dual-track review completed—further revision possible, stable use (still open to renewed review). “Merged” must not be presented as completed review;
- **Public source**: Link only content that is already public;
- **Decision record**: Link the corresponding number in [DECISION_LOG.en.md](DECISION_LOG.en.md) or a separate file.

## III. Registration Rules

1. Each nonpublic submission receives only one private random receipt number, which never enters this table;
2. Each authorized public “comment × principle provision” receives one public sub-item number; use I00 for overall comments;
3. Append supplements, corrections, and reconsideration to the original public sub-item number or establish an explicit association;
4. Mark identical content submitted through multiple channels as duplicate merged; do not count it twice;
5. Do not create a public record when unpublished comments are withdrawn. For published comments, mark withdrawal or cessation of further use under authorization and safety rules;
6. During a privacy suspension, do not add summaries that might expose identity;
7. Preserve dates of status changes; do not conceal history through silent overwriting;
8. Unverified public comments must not be labeled as formal expert or worker participation;
9. Store public registers and private identity mappings in separate files or systems.

## IV. Statistical Definitions

Each round summary separately counts the following aggregates. Combine or withhold categories where combinations could enable re-identification:

- Formally verified experts;
- Formally verified workers;
- Unverified public participants;
- Unverified nonpublic participants (safe aggregates only);
- Withdrawals;
- Duplicates merged;
- Invalid submissions.

The first four are mutually exclusive primary participant categories. One person enters only one primary track for the same principle in the same round. Named, pseudonymous, unattributed text, and internal-only handling are publication-mode attributes. They may be reported separately as subsets of the above categories, but must not be added again to participant totals.

Views, likes, reposts, and simple emoji reactions do not count as reviewers. When one person submits multiple comments, report participant counts and comment counts separately.

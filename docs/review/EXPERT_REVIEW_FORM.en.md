# Expert Review Form

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](EXPERT_REVIEW_FORM.md)

> Template version: v0.2-draft<br>
> Check the round announcement before formally submitting. **Do not upload the nonpublic portion of this file to GitHub.**

This form is for reviewers who have completed the application/invitation process and joined a specific round. It does not replace the [expert participation application form](EXPERT_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md).

This form has two independent parts:

- Part A contains substantive review comments and may be published as authorized;
- Part B contains contact, verification, and authorization information and must be submitted only through the nonpublic channel designated in the round announcement.

Upon receipt, the project assigns both parts the same private random receipt number. Only after the participant authorizes publication and a risk check is completed will the project generate a separate public record number for the public text. Participants submitting publicly must delete Part B and must not paste private receipt numbers.

## 3. Recording Requirements

- A decision may link to multiple public items or aggregated internal evidence, but must not conceal objections. Where one submission addresses different provisions with different outcomes, record them separately.
- Use different IDs for separate decisions on the same provision.
- Retain the original record and append new results when a decision is reconsidered or corrected.
- Typo, link, and formatting corrections may be briefly noted in the changelog; do not present them as substantive review decisions.
- Every formal amendment must specify the applicable version; do not automatically transfer an old review to new text.
- Decision records must not contain unauthorized names, contact details, precise organizations, or verification materials.
- Also create a project-level DR for major changes to core principles, licensing, or governance structure. Routine formatting corrections and individual review dispositions do not require duplicate DR records.

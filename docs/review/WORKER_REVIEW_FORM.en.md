# Worker Review Form

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](WORKER_REVIEW_FORM.md)

> Template version: v0.2-draft<br>
> You may answer only the questions relevant to you; completing every field is not required. **Do not post Part B of this document to GitHub.**

This form is for reviewers who have completed the application/invitation process and joined a specific round. It does not replace the [worker participation application form](WORKER_REVIEW_APPLICATION_FORM.en.md).

This form consists of:

- Part A: Review comments, which may be published according to your choices;
- Part B: Contact details, lightweight verification, and publication authorization, which must use only the non-public channel specified in the round announcement.

If you need transcription, translation, or language editing, you must confirm before submission that the content has not been changed.

The project generates a random receipt ID for non-public submissions. Do not paste it into GitHub. After you consent to publication and the risk check is complete, the project will generate a separate public record ID.

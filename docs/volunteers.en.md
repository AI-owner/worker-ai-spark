# Volunteer Sign-up

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](volunteers.md)

We welcome people who are interested in Worker AI Spark and would like to help build the project over the long term. Programming skills are not required. You can contribute according to your interests, available time, and strengths.

## Work you might take part in

1. **Building the project across its stages:** contribute over time to discussions, content organization, collaboration, and other development work.
2. **Connecting with workers and understanding current conditions:** establish and maintain contact on the project's behalf with willing workers, learning how AI is affecting their work and lives.
3. **Understanding workers' hopes for AI:** listen to the help they want and the problems they worry about, and bring their ideas and needs back to the project.
4. **Spreading the word:** explain the project's goals, progress, and ways to participate so more interested people can learn about it and join.
5. **Future work:** take on other suitable work as new needs arise.

We can discuss what you would like to do; no one needs to take on every task.

Agree on times and ways to communicate with workers. The project will protect their privacy and will not publish identities, workplaces, contact details, conversations, or other related information without their explicit consent.

## How to apply

Email [worker.ai.spark@gmail.com](mailto:worker.ai.spark@gmail.com), with an optional subject such as “Volunteer application.” Briefly include two things:

1. **A short introduction:** what to call you (a real name or pseudonym is fine), and roughly what work you do or what you know about.
2. **Time available each month:** an estimate of the hours you can realistically contribute. There is no minimum.

No formal résumé or identity documents are needed. Application information will not be shared publicly without your consent.

**If you have not received a reply within one week, you may not be a fit for the current work arrangements. The project will contact you by email when there is a suitable fit.**

## How contributions are recorded

Work actually done and reasonable suggestions that are adopted will be recorded and assigned contribution values. Public credit can use your real name, a pseudonym, or anonymity, as you prefer. Contribution values are honorary recognition of “working for the working class”; they carry no material benefits. See the [contribution honor roll and explanation](../CONTRIBUTORS.en.md).

If you only want to share your hopes, you can write directly to the [workers' mailbox](../README.en.md#workers-mailbox) without volunteering.

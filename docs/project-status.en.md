## How to participate now

- **Volunteer:** Interested people can contribute long-term across project stages: worker contact, understanding AI's actual effects and workers' hopes, outreach, and future work. Email a short introduction and your available time each month; see [sign-up details](volunteers.en.md).
- **Write to the workers' mailbox:** Send hopes for AI and the project to [worker.ai.spark@gmail.com](mailto:worker.ai.spark@gmail.com). Nothing related to you will be published without explicit consent; see [the mailbox](../README.en.md#workers-mailbox).
- **Leave one comment:** In [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/EastForce/worker-ai-spark/discussions), identify the question or passage and what you support, oppose, or think is missing. A few sentences suffice; personal experience and proof of identity are not required.
- **Report an error or suggest an edit:** Use the existing [Issue forms](https://github.com/EastForce/worker-ai-spark/issues/new/choose) or a Pull Request. See [the guide](../CONTRIBUTING.en.md).
- **Without GitHub:** Email brief feedback or a de-identified overview. Maintainers see the sender's address; email is neither anonymous nor end-to-end encrypted. Confirm final text, attribution, and licensing separately before publication.

Public comments show GitHub usernames. Submit only material suitable for publication, not identity documents, contact details, full contracts, pay records, or identifiable third-party materials. Email is also unsuitable for sending such sensitive originals directly; see [email guidance](../CONTRIBUTING.en.md#4-email-submissions).

**Feedback handling:** Ordinary comments are not formal reviews or endorsement of the whole project. Record adoption, changes, or reasons for non-adoption in the relevant discussion or version notes. Receipt is not acceptance. Individual replies or fixed response times are not promised. Volunteer applicants who hear nothing within one week may not fit current arrangements; the project will contact them when suitable.

**Contribution records:** Actual work and adopted reasonable suggestions receive contribution values, with real-name, pseudonymous, or anonymous credit chosen by the contributor. Values are honorary records only and carry no material benefits; see [the honor roll](../CONTRIBUTORS.en.md).

## When formal review can begin

Formal round notices will be published only after responsible people, handling arrangements, reviewed versions, round dates, and working participation channels are in place. Until then, collect no formal identity-verification materials and do not label ordinary comments formal review results.

The existing [public invitation](https://github.com/EastForce/worker-ai-spark/discussions/4) can receive ordinary feedback, but does not establish that a formal round has begun. All [opening conditions](review/README.en.md#i-current-status) and a formal round notice are required.

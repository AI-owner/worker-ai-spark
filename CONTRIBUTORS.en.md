# Contribution Honor Roll and Contributor Records

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](CONTRIBUTORS.md)

> This document publishes contribution values and preserves records of real contributions. Contributors choose real-name, pseudonymous, or anonymous public credit. Do not invent contributors or values.

---

## 1. Contribution types

The project respects and records real labor. Contributions include more than code:

- **Initiation:** proposing the project and undertaking early maintenance.
- **Core writing:** drafting or revising the manifesto, principles, and evaluation questions.
- **Theory:** proposing, arguing, challenging, or revising foundational theory and core papers.
- **Content review:** reviewing documents and identifying errors and omissions.
- **Evaluation design:** designing and revising questions and scoring criteria.
- **Technical development:** code, scripts, repository structure, and automation.
- **Translation:** making the project accessible in other languages.
- **Community maintenance:** organizing discussion, answering questions, and supporting newcomers.
- **Worker contact:** maintaining contact with willing workers and understanding AI's impact over time.
- **Documenting expectations:** listening to and organizing workers' hopes and needs for AI.
- **Project outreach:** explaining goals, progress, and participation routes.
- **Suggestions:** reasonable ideas adopted by the project.
- **Corrections:** identifying and fixing factual, citation, or formatting errors.

Other actual work arising as the project develops will also be recorded according to its contribution.

---

## 2. Identity choices

Contributors choose how publicly to identify themselves:

- Real name.
- GitHub username.
- Pseudonym.
- Anonymous contribution.

The project must respect that choice. Contribution summaries include only suitable public content. Publishing a contribution value does not authorize disclosure of workers' identities, contact details, or conversations.

---

## 3. Contribution values and giving back

Volunteers' actual labor and reasonable suggestions that are adopted will be recorded and assigned a “contribution value.” Work across project stages, worker contact, understanding hopes for AI, outreach, and future tasks are needed contributions, just like code, writing, and translation.

Maintainers determine values from actual effort, completed work, and help to the project, record a brief basis, and publish using the contributor's chosen name format. Report omissions or errors through the [project mailbox](mailto:worker.ai.spark@gmail.com).

**Contribution values are solely honorary recognition within the project of “working for the working class.” They carry no material benefits and represent no money, shares, tokens, or redeemable rights.**

If the project eventually trains a Workers' AI and has sufficient resources, it will consider the AI's views and actual resources and decide through public discussion how to give back to contributors. There is no set timing, form, or amount, and no promise of economic returns.

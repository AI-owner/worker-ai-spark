# Code of Conduct

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

> This code applies to this repository and related public discussion spaces. The project seeks respectful discussion in which criticism and correction are possible.

---

## 1. Prohibited conduct

The following are not permitted:

- **Personal attacks:** insults, ridicule, or disparagement directed at individuals.
- **Harassment:** persistent unwanted contact, intimidation, or coordinated targeting.
- **Threats:** threats to personal safety, employment, or security.
- **Hate speech:** hatred or denigration based on ethnicity, gender, region, disability, religion, background, or similar characteristics.
- **Discrimination:** denying the value of someone's labor, knowledge, or eligibility to participate because of identity.
- **Exposing others' private information:** disclosing names, workplaces, phone numbers, addresses, or similar information without consent.
- **Impersonation:** falsely presenting oneself as a worker, expert, maintainer, or institution.
- **Deliberately spreading false information.**
- **Humiliating ordinary people in the name of class standpoint:** attacking individuals as insufficiently conscious or enlightened.
- **Personality cults:** treating an initiator, maintainer, or model as an unquestionable authority.
- **Coordinated personal attacks over political or theoretical disagreements.**

---

## 2. Protected conduct

The following should be protected and encouraged:

- **Theoretical criticism:** criticism of theories, views, and projects.
- **Questioning factual claims:** evidence-based correction.
- **Reasonable criticism of the project's initiator and maintainers.**
- **Minority opinions:** views that differ from the majority and are supported by facts and reasoning.
- **Evidence-based disagreement.**

---

## 3. Enforcement principles

- Recognize differences, tensions, and differing interests among workers rather than erasing them.
- Working-class emancipation must not rest on discrimination against or oppression of other groups.
- This code grants no person, organization, or model immunity from criticism.
- Enforcement itself must remain open to public discussion and revision.

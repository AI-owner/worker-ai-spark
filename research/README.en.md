## Possible topics

- Labor relations, power, and bargaining capacity.
- Algorithmic management, automation, and employment.
- AI ethics, fairness, and explainability.
- Labor law and occupational health.
- Workers' movement history and traditions of labor thought.
- Platform work, care work, and informal employment.

---

## Inclusion principles (not yet implemented)

- Primarily use public, verifiable sources.
- Record authors, publication dates, sources, and accessible links.
- Identify the scope and limitations of findings.
- Do not present a single study as definitive.
- Welcome additions and criticism from researchers of different theoretical backgrounds.

# Labor Knowledge Base

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](README.md)

> This is a **reserved directory**. Systematic construction of the knowledge base has not begun.

---

## Goal

Gradually preserve public knowledge about labor for AI and public use, including:

- Labor history and the history of workers' movements.
- Labor law and social protection systems, using public, lawfully available sources.
- Occupational safety and health.
- Factory systems, platform work, and care work.
- The history of labor technologies and the effects of automation.
- Workers' literature and records of ordinary workers' experience.

---

## Construction principles (not yet implemented)

- Organize only **public, lawfully available** sources; do not upload unauthorized material.
- Record sources, selection criteria, and scope of applicability.
- Respect privacy and copyright; do not appropriate workers' experience without compensation in the name of Workers' AI.
- Mark unverifiable, contested, or regionally limited content.
- Collect workers' experience with informed consent, privacy protection, and respect for anonymity.

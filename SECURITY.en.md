# Security

> **Chinese version:** [阅读中文版](SECURITY.md)

> This document explains priority security and privacy risks and how to report them.

---

## 1. Priority risks

- **Privacy leaks:** disclosure of workers' names, workplaces, contact details, addresses, employee IDs, or similar information.
- **Worker identification:** re-identification of people in anonymous cases.
- **Disclosure of internal business information:** unauthorized chat records, internal documents, or product secrets.
- **Malicious submissions:** fabricated, manipulative, or harmful content in questions, cases, or the knowledge base.
- **Prompt injection:** attempts to manipulate evaluation systems or documents through submitted content.
- **Tampering with evaluations:** changing scores or deleting unfavorable records.
- **Misuse of the project's name:** making commitments, raising funds, or exerting pressure in its name.
- **Malware and insecure code:** scripts containing vulnerabilities or malicious functionality.
- **Harassment or retaliation against contributors**, especially workers submitting real cases.

---

## 2. Protecting contributors

- **Never require contributors to disclose sensitive information in public Issues.**
- Workers may submit real cases they personally experienced, directly know about, or have reasonable factual grounds for (see [CONTRIBUTING.en.md](CONTRIBUTING.en.md)). They may choose real-name, pseudonymous, publicly anonymous, or internal-only handling. GitHub is not an anonymous channel. De-identified initial information unsuitable for publication can first be emailed to the official mailbox. Email is also neither guaranteed anonymous nor end-to-end encrypted; maintainers can see the sender's address. Names, workplaces, contact details, original materials, and verbatim opinions each require separate explicit permission before publication. De-identify, restrict, or postpone disclosure of third-party information, internal materials, and identifying details that could lead to retaliation.
- Regardless of anonymity, **do not** submit others' private information or unauthorized internal material to the public repository.

---

## 3. Reporting security issues

Report privacy and security vulnerabilities to the official mailbox:

> **[worker.ai.spark@gmail.com](mailto:worker.ai.spark@gmail.com)**
>
> **Do not** post details of unresolved privacy or security vulnerabilities in public Issues. Suggested subject: `[Security Report] Brief topic`. Start with a minimal description; do not attach identity documents, full contracts, pay records, third-party private information, or other high-risk originals.

The same mailbox accepts theory submissions. Suggested subject: `[Theory Submission] Title`. See the [email submission guidance](CONTRIBUTING.en.md#4-email-submissions). Distinguish submissions and security reports by their subject prefixes.

The initiator supplied this contact address; changes will be recorded in [CHANGELOG.en.md](CHANGELOG.en.md). Email is neither anonymous nor end-to-end encrypted. Sending an email does not authorize publication of identities, text, or attachments.

---

## 4. Outside scope

This document does not require disclosure of private information unrelated to the project or authorize anyone to suppress reasonable criticism and factual discussion in the name of security.
